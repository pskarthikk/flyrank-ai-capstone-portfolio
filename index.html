FLYRANK ML INTERNSHIP · ML-02

Refresh / Content Opportunity Scoring

SHIPPED

Which content pages should be prioritized for review so editors can focus on pages with the highest potential for improvement?

Problem

Editors cannot manually inspect every page. The goal was to rank pages so a small review queue contains more likely opportunities.

What I did

Explored the starter data, built a readable baseline, compared a hand-written rule with ML models, and evaluated the ranking with Precision@K.

What came of it

The starter experiment reached 0.740 Precision@50 with Random Forest versus 0.240 for the hand-written rule.

03

Honest story

I built this portfolio because I wanted a place where I could show the work instead of repeatedly saying that I am learning AI and machine learning.

The biggest help from AI was not a single code snippet. It was having a partner that could turn a vague idea into a sequence of small decisions, explain unfamiliar implementation details, and help me iterate quickly. That let me spend more time deciding what the project should prove.

The limitation was just as useful: AI can make a site look finished before the underlying claims have been checked. Metrics, links, edge cases, and what actually happened in the project still need human verification. I learned to treat AI output as a draft and the evidence as the source of truth.

— Karthikk
